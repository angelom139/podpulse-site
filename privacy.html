Privacy Policy

PodPulse Publisher · Last updated: September 28, 2026

PodPulse Publisher ("the App") is a private publishing tool operated by PodPulse. It schedules and uploads short-form videos created by PodPulse to PodPulse's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. It is not distributed to, or usable by, the general public.

Information the App accesses

The App accesses only the accounts of its operator, and only after the operator authorizes it:

  • Google / YouTube: permission to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel (the YouTube Data API youtube.upload scope), plus the authorization tokens needed to do so.
  • TikTok and Instagram: basic profile information and the authorization tokens required to publish videos on the operator's behalf.

The App does not collect information about any other person, does not track visitors, and does not use cookies or analytics.

How information is used

Access is used solely to publish videos the operator has scheduled, to the operator's own accounts. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or any secondary purpose.

Google user data

PodPulse Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to upload the operator's own videos to the operator's own YouTube channel; it is not sold, shared, or used for any other purpose.

Storage and security

Authorization tokens, videos, captions, and posting history are stored locally on the operator's own computer. No data is sent to any third party other than the platform being posted to.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data. Videos and captions are transmitted only to the platform selected, at the moment of publishing.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the operator's computer until the operator deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time: Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access; TikTok → Settings → Security → Apps and services; Instagram/Facebook → Settings → Apps and websites. To request deletion of any data, contact the address below.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children.
